What the kit protects

The kit contains the key that decrypts your stored bank tokens, plus your Plaid credentials, all wrapped in a passphrase you choose.

Without the key, your ledger still has every transaction, but each bank must be linked again. Keep the kit off this computer, and keep the passphrase separate from it.
