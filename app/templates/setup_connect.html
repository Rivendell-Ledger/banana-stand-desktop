Get your Plaid keys

Plaid is what reads your bank. It has a free personal plan, and your keys stay on this computer.

  1. 1Open dashboard.plaid.com and create a developer account. Choose Personal use — not Business, not App user.
  2. 2When it asks for a website, any valid URL you control works — a personal site, a blog, or your LinkedIn profile.
  3. 3Open Developers → Keys and copy the Client ID and the production secret.
  4. 4Paste both below. The app tests them with Plaid before saving.

Never invent a company or an app name on the Plaid form. Personal use is the correct answer. The website field is required.
