Why this matters

Your bank connections are protected by an encryption key stored in this app's data folder. The nightly backups copy the database but not that key.

If the key is lost, nothing in your ledger disappears — but every bank has to be linked again. The recovery kit is one small file that avoids that.

  • 1Choose a passphrase you will remember. It encrypts the kit; nobody can open the file without it.
  • 2Download the kit and move it somewhere off this computer — a USB drive, your password manager, or cloud storage.
  • 3Keep the passphrase separate from the file. The kit alone is useless to anyone who finds it.

Do not save the kit inside the Banana Stand data folder. It belongs somewhere a disk failure cannot take with it.
